# Proposed Evaluation — Not Completed Results

## 1. Live integration and source support

Run at least one aviation and one unrelated industry case. Preserve logs, model IDs, timestamps and costs. Have a reviewer open every cited source and classify each important claim as supported, partially supported, unsupported or unverifiable. Report the denominator and disagreements; do not hide excluded or failed cases.

Test a generic organization, a named organization, a sparse-evidence emerging technology, conflicting sources and a deliberately excluded source. Verify the exported Strategist inputs contain only approved evidence. Treat URL presence and source support as separate outcomes.

## 2. Strategy usefulness

Ask experienced reviewers to assess problem fit, alternatives, evidence transfer, feasibility, stakeholder coverage and whether the proposed KPI could support a decision. Compare FORGE with a standard one-prompt AI answer using the same input. Blind reviewers to the generating approach where practical. No superiority claim is justified by this proposal alone.

## 3. Human review behavior

Observe consenting users completing the task. Measure source-opening behavior, time to inspect evidence, ability to identify a seeded unsupported finding, usefulness of rating reasons and whether they understand Unknown. Collect qualitative feedback on workload and trust. Do not infer usefulness from completion time alone.

## 4. Critic quality

Build a small set of strategy briefs with known unsupported claims, omitted stakeholders and weak KPI definitions. Measure whether the critic identifies each issue and whether it invents new problems. Compare with an independent human review. A separate model or provider may reduce some shared blind spots, but requires testing.

## Reporting

Record failures as well as successes. Publish aggregate measurements and anonymized examples only with appropriate data rights. Update the résumé and case study only after observations exist; all current business benefits remain hypotheses.
